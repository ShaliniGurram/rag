The next morning, Aanya decided to search the backyard, following a faint memory her grandmother once mentioned.
Behind the mango tree, she discovered a narrow stone path, half buried under leaves.
As she followed it, the air turned cooler, quieter.
At the end of the path stood a small abandoned cottage—and on the door, the same pressed flower symbol from the letter.
Aanya felt her heart race.
She wasn’t just holding someone’s letter… she was stepping into their story.
